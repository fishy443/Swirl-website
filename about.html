Who are we?

Boba Bloom started with one extremely important question: why should drinks be boring?

We wanted to create a fictional bubble tea shop that feels colourful, playful and a little bit chaotic.

✦ Our mission:

Make every drink feel like a tiny celebration.

Three things we care about

01 — Fun

Drinks should be exciting, not just something you grab on the way home.

02 — Flavour

Sweet, fruity, creamy or refreshing — there is always something new to try.

03 — Creativity

Every part of Boba Bloom is designed to be colourful and completely original.
